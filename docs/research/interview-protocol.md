# Today view — interview protocol

Use with the paper prototype [`prototype/today.html`](../../prototype/today.html) or the running app (`npm run dev`).

Goal: learn whether one screen can hold **routines + untimed checklist + timed events** without feeling noisy, before we build booking or Google sync.

## Recruiting

Five people who already juggle at least two of: a calendar, a to-do list, a habit tracker. Exclude professional schedulers and Calendly power users for this round.

Suggested mix: new parent, office worker with a commute, student, fitness-oriented adult, someone who hosts friends often.

## Script (25 minutes)

1. **Warm-up (3 min)** — How do you plan a typical weekday morning? Which apps?
2. **Task A (5 min)** — “It’s Tuesday. Mark your morning routine done and check off two daily items.” Watch where they tap first.
3. **Task B (5 min)** — “You need to add a dentist appointment Thursday at 2pm. Where would you put that vs ‘drink water’?”
4. **Task C (5 min)** — “A friend wants to grab coffee. What would you send them today? What would you want this app to send instead?”
5. **Concept (5 min)** — Show the three layers (routine / checklist / clock). Ask what they would delete.
6. **Willingness (2 min)** — Would Google Calendar inside this app be worth paying ~$6/month? Booking link?

## Notes to capture

- First tap on Today
- Confusion between untimed checklist vs timed routine
- Whether they look for a month grid
- Booking: instant confirm vs request-to-approve
- Quotes on “one app vs current stack”
